## Brands and trademarks

The game is set in Rio Grande do Sul, and local brands are part of that
landscape: Farmácias São João, Charrua, Fruki, the colours of Grêmio and
Internacional, and others. They appear because they belong to the region's
everyday life and culture.

- All trademarks, logos and brand names are the property of their respective
  owners. Their appearance does not imply any affiliation with or endorsement
  by them.
- They are not covered by the project's licences: the GPL and CC BY-SA apply
  to our code and our own artwork, not to third-party marks.
- Prefer brand material the owner has published or that is in the public
  domain (e.g. text logos on Wikimedia Commons), and note its source in
  CREDITS.md.
- If a brand owner asks for their mark to be removed, it will be removed.
