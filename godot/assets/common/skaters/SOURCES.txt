SKATEBOARDER HUMAN SOURCE ASSETS

Same source library as the pedestrian pack. Skateboards, headphones and motion are original for this delivery.

MAKEHUMAN GRAPHICAL ASSET SOURCES

The pedestrian characters adapt CC0 MakeHuman graphical assets. This includes
the hm08 anatomical base mesh, native skin weights, age/body/facial morphs,
core fitted garments and footwear, hair cards, eye meshes, eyebrow meshes,
and source diffuse/normal surface maps. Added role accessories and cloth
panels were built for this batch. Character surfaces were recoloured, atlas
packed, and combined; geometry was fitted and reduced, then rigged/exported
with a shared Mixamo-named skeleton and animated for this pack.
