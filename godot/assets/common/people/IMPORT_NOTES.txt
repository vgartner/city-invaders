PORTO ALEGRE PEDESTRIANS — 16 CHARACTER PACK

CONTENTS
16 self-contained glTF 2.0 binary characters, named ped_01_... to ped_16_....
Individual previews, a horizontal side-by-side contact sheet, an additional
4x4 overview, roster.json, and Pedestrians_Validation.json are included.

VARIETY
8 women and 8 men, all adults. Four young adults, four adults, four middle-aged
adults, and four elderly adults. Statures range from 1.56 to 1.88 metres.
The roster combines slim, average, heavy, tall and short builds; distinct
complexions, age-specific faces, haircuts and hair colours; office clothing,
students with backpacks, a courier with insulated bag, street vendor with
apron, construction worker with hardhat/vest, jogger, retirees, tourist with
camera/cap, summer dress, jeans/T-shirt and a gaúcho-style striped poncho.
Small accessories include glasses, phone, handbag, shopping bag, umbrella,
and watch. The poncho is the requested cold-day exception to warm-weather wear.

TECHNICAL
- One skinned mesh, one primitive, one material per GLB.
- 9,344 to 14,255 triangles, including hair and accessories.
- Metres, +Y up, facing -Z; ground-contact soles at Y=0 and feet at the origin.
- Three embedded 2048x2048 PNG PBR maps: base colour, tangent-space normal,
  metallic/roughness. Roughness is G and metallic B.
- One padded atlas combines body, outfit, hair, footwear, eyes and accessories.
  UV seams are retained; deliberate UV reuse on repeated accessory surfaces.
- Hair and eyebrows use alpha masking within the same material. Preserve
  MASK, alphaCutoff=0.35, doubleSided=true and the supplied texture sampler.
- No lights, cameras, ground plane, scene background, or external textures.

SHARED RIG
All characters use the same 52-bone canonical humanoid rig with mixamorig:
bone names, identical hierarchy, local bind transforms and inverse bind
matrices. Fingers and toes are included. The bind pose is a relaxed A-pose.
An outer stature wrapper scales the skeleton uniformly for different heights;
keep that wrapper scale when importing. The mesh node is a scene-root node,
and the stature wrapper is a separate scene root carrying the skeleton.
Do not reset wrapper scales without baking the corresponding skin transforms.
Humanoid retargeting may require your engine's bone mapping configuration;
these assets have not been uploaded to the Mixamo web service.

ANIMATION — IDENTICAL SET IN EVERY FILE, SAMPLED AT 30 FPS
idle      3.0 seconds    loop
walk      1.0 second     loop, in place
sprint    0.6 seconds    loop, in place
 die      2.0 seconds    one-shot backward collapse, hold final pose

All rotation tracks and timestamps are shared across the 16 files. The hip
translation receives a small per-character floor-contact adjustment so shoes,
physiques and carried items do not sink into the floor. Idle, walk and sprint
endpoints match. Loop intent is recorded in extras.loop; set repeat playback
for those clips in your engine. glTF does not provide a universal runtime loop
flag. Use one-shot playback and hold/clamp for die. No optional clips included.
Set locomotion speed, collisions, navigation, ragdoll behavior and gameplay
in your engine. Clothing and carried accessories are skinned; the files do
not require a runtime cloth simulator.

VALIDATION
Every GLB passes Khronos glTF Validator with 0 errors and 0 warnings.
Independent checks read the delivered binaries and verify:
triangle budget; real-world height; feet origin; texture dimensions/embedding;
finite geometry; nondegenerate triangles; unit normals/tangents; UV range;
normalized weights; valid joints; matching skeleton and inverse binds;
matching clip rotation/timing data; exact clip names; 30 fps samples;
loop seams; and finite, nondegenerate, floor-contact poses at every frame.
Pedestrians_Validation.json includes per-file SHA-256 hashes and results.
The contact sheet and previews were rendered from the delivered GLBs through
Three.js GLTFLoader. Preview lighting and background are absent from the GLBs.
